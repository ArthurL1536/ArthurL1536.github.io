Tutorial

Welcome to Stargazer! On this website, you will explore stars, planets, and other celestial objects.

First, hit the play button. This will take you to a picture of the Milkey Way, with little star icons.

Click on one of those, and you'll be taken to the star's solar system.

There you will be able to find information about the star, its planets, asteroids, and sometimes even spaceships! Enjoy!
